Device Security Policy

Purpose: This policy is designed to protect Company-owned devices from unauthorized access, misuse, and loss. It outlines the criteria for password requirements, device encryption, screen lock requirements, software update requirements, and approved software policy. The policy also includes guidelines for reporting lost or stolen devices.

Definitions:

  * Device: A Company-owned device that is used for work-related activities, such as email, office management, and communication.
  * Security Policy: A set of rules and guidelines for managing and protecting Company-owned devices.

Password Requirements:

  * All passwords must be changed at least once every 6 months and be at least 12 characters long.
  * Passwords must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one digit, and one special character (e.g., !@#$%^&*).

Device Encryption:

  * All devices must be encrypted when not in use, except for USB drives that are used for external storage.
  * If a device is lost or stolen, it must be immediately disconnected from the network and all data encrypted.
  * Devices should be encrypted in storage to ensure data is protected even when not in use.

Screen Lock Requirements:

  * All devices must be set to require a password or biometric data when the screen is unlocked.
  * If a device is lost or stolen, it must be immediately disconnected from the network and the screen locked.
  * Screen lock options must include a password, fingerprint scan, or facial recognition.

Software Update Requirements:

  * All devices must be updated to the latest version of any software, including browsers, email clients, and other applications, at least once every 3 months.
  * Updates should be performed remotely to ensure data is not compromised during the update process.
  * Updates should be tested on a non-production device before being applied to the affected devices.

Approved Software Policy:

  * Only software that has been pre-approved by the IT department may be used on Company-owned devices.
  * Software must be downloaded from a company-issued USB drive or secure website.
  * Software must be used in accordance with the approved software policy and any applicable company policies.
  * Software must be removed from Company-owned devices when no longer in use.

Lost or Stolen Device Reporting:

  * If a lost or stolen device is found, it must be immediately reported to the IT department.
  * The IT department must be notified of the device's location, make, and model.
  * Contact information for the device owner must be provided.
  * The device must be turned off and remotely wiped to prevent unauthorized access.

Review and Approval:

  * This policy must be reviewed at least once every year by the IT department.
  * Any changes to the policy must be reviewed and approved by the appropriate authority.

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
