MLOps Handbook: Mastering Model Lifecycle Management in the Age of AI

Introduction:

In the rapidly evolving world of AI, model development and deployment have become increasingly complex, requiring a robust framework for lifecycle management. The MLOps Handbook presents a comprehensive overview of these critical processes, including model lifecycle management, Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) for ML, monitoring, version control, and deployment standards. This handbook is designed for all stakeholders involved in model development, including data scientists, engineers, and business leaders, to ensure a seamless transition from ideation to production.
